Todoist helps you keep track of everything you need to do, at home and at work. With this app, your Homey becomes part of that list.

Let your home add tasks for you, like a reminder to empty the dishwasher as soon as it finishes. Start Flows when a task is completed and check whether a task is still open before a routine runs. Add the widget to your Homey dashboard to see a project at a glance and tick off tasks right from there.
